Professional Profile & Philosophy

Strategic alignment of academic rigour, statutory environmental frameworks, and geospatial data modeling to build sustainable, disaster-resilient communities.

As a licensed Registered Environmental Planner (EnP) and graduate of Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science from Caraga State University, my career focus centers on the intersection of geospatial intelligence, statutory urban/regional planning, and environmental protection. My academic and professional training has equipped me with practical mastery in Geographic Information Systems (GIS) mapping, Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) under Presidential Decree 1586, and Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) formulation in line with the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) guidelines.

Throughout my fieldwork and laboratory research in the Caraga Region—one of the country's most ecologically critical and resource-rich territories—I have led spatial vulnerability evaluations, hydrological watershed modeling, and baseline environmental quality monitoring. I believe that sound environmental planning is rooted in measurable spatial data, rigorous multi-criteria analysis, and authentic, inclusive stakeholder dialogue.
